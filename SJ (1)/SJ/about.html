Environmental Benefits of LPG & CNG

Alternative fuels like LPG and CNG provide cleaner, economical and sustainable transportation solutions compared to conventional petrol and diesel fuels.

🔥

Environmental Benefits of LPG

  • Lower CO₂ emissions compared to petrol and diesel, helping reduce greenhouse gases.
  • Produces lower NOₓ, CO and hydrocarbon emissions, improving air quality.
  • Produces very little particulate matter, reducing smoke and soot pollution.
  • Contains almost no sulfur or lead, reducing harmful SO₂ emissions.
🌱

Environmental Benefits of CNG

  • Produces 20–30% less CO₂ emissions than petrol and diesel.
  • Generates almost zero particulate emissions, making it one of the cleanest fuels.
  • Reduces NOₓ, CO and unburned hydrocarbon emissions.
  • Creates less engine noise and reduces noise pollution.
